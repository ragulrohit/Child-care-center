Our Facilities

Spaces designed for little people

Every corner of STACKLY is built at child height, with safety, comfort and curiosity in mind.

🏫

Safe Classrooms

Soft-edged furniture, rounded corners and child-height everything. Rooms are bright, airy and clearly zoned for quiet and active play.

🎒

Indoor Play Area

A weather-proof play zone with soft mats, climbing frames and ball pits — perfect for burning off energy any day of the year.

🌲

Outdoor Playground

A shaded, fenced garden with slides, swings and a mud kitchen where children can explore nature safely every day.

📖

Reading Corner

A snug nook stuffed with picture books, cushions and puppets that turns story time into the best part of the day.

💤

Nap & Rest Area

Quiet, dimly lit rooms with individual cots, fresh linen and gentle lullabies so every child can recharge.

🎨

Creative Learning Room

A studio full of paint, clay, fabric and recycled treasures where mess is welcome and imagination runs wild.

🍽

Child-Friendly Dining Area

Tables built for tiny hands and tummies, serving fresh, balanced meals with allergy-aware menus.

🧼

Hygiene & Safety

Child-height sinks, sanitising stations and daily deep-cleaning routines keep little hands and spaces germ-free.
